# Requirements Kit

Five skills written by Cheatcode for the jobs business analysts do every release and that open-source packs cover thinly: requirements-lint finds ambiguous, untestable, undefined, compound and conflicting statements and missing non-functional and edge cases, with a proposed rewrite or question for each; acceptance-criteria writes Gherkin scenarios or EARS statements with boundary, negative, permission and dependency-failure cases; traceability-matrix builds the objective to requirement to story to test matrix as CSV and reports every gap; uat-plan produces the plan, business-facing scripts and the sign-off record; brd-draft turns workshop notes and transcripts into a structured first draft whose open questions are impossible to miss. None of them invents numbers, owners or rules: unknowns become questions.

Published by Cheatcode under the MIT license. Written from the role research in the Cheatcode repository (docs/research).
