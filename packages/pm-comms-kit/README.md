# PM Comms Kit

Two skills written by Cheatcode for communication chores product managers repeat every week. release-notes-house-voice learns a style card from the team's past release notes before drafting from commits, pull requests or tickets, and lists what it left out and what engineering must confirm. comment-replies classifies open comments on a spec, drafts grounded replies and doc edits in the author's voice, holds back what only the author can decide, and posts nothing until approved.
